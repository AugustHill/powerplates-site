The cause

Chili for a cause

The Chili Cook-Off is a fundraiser for the Pinellas Toys for Tots Foundation. Come hungry and thirsty, vote for your favorite chili, and help provide a Christmas for less fortunate children while enjoying the company of friends.

The need keeps growing. In 2025, Toys for Tots in Pinellas County supported 9,551 children and distributed 35,103 toys. Learn more about Toys for Tots in Pinellas County.

$3,000 raised for Toys for Tots at last year's cook-off
How it works

Come hungry. Vote for your favorite.

1

Get your ticket

Your $25 ticket comes with bowls and spoons to devour the chilis, a complimentary beer, and a ballot to cast your vote. Pay online or at the door.

2

Taste the chili

Sample a variety of chili recipes from talented cooks. Every chili is displayed anonymously, so it all comes down to flavor.

3

Cast your vote

Vote for your favorite chili. The competition will be fierce, but the flavors will be even bolder. Seven awards are up for grabs.

Good to know

What does my ticket include?

Your $25 ticket comes with bowls and spoons to devour the chilis, a complimentary beer, and a ballot to cast your vote.

Can I pay at the event?

Yes. You can pay at the door, or pay online for convenience.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are permitted on the covered patio and while you are inside ordering your brews.

Can kids come?

Guests under 21 are welcome with a parent or legal guardian.

How do I become a cook?

Register to cook on the Eventbrite page. Cook spots are extremely limited and will go fast.

How do I become a sponsor?

All sponsors are judges of the chili and get two tickets to the event. Your $300 sponsorship is a check written directly to Toys for Tots, so it is pure charity. Contact Derrick at derrick.mckenzie@kw.com or 440-752-9616.

Can I donate a gift basket for the silent auction?

Yes, please! Contact Derrick at derrick.mckenzie@kw.com or 440-752-9616.

I can't make the event. Can I still donate?

You sure can. In the ticket info on Eventbrite there is a donation option.

What is Power Plates?

Power Plates is a networking group that meets monthly at various restaurants across the area for lunch. We also do cool fundraiser events like this one to help local charities and people in need. Check us out on Facebook or read more about us.
