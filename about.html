About us

What Power Plates is all about

Power Plates started with a simple idea: the best part of community isn't the event, it's the table. We bring people together over shared meals, good books, and honest conversation, and we celebrate the members who show up for each other, week after week.

Our mission

Connection over a meal

We believe food is one of the easiest ways to build real community. Every event, dinner, and recommendation we share is in service of getting more people around the table together.

Our values

Honest & welcoming

Our restaurant reviews and book picks are genuine, written by members, for members. No sponsorships, no filler. Just what we actually loved.

Our people

Member-led

Power Plates is run by the people who show up. From potlucks to book club, members shape what this community becomes next.
