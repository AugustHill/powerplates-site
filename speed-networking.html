A note from Derrick

Thank you for being there

Thank you for joining us at Ignite Connections Speed Networking on Thursday, November 5, at the Scottish American Society of Dunedin. Whether you came with a stack of business cards or just an open mind, you helped make the room what it was: full of energy, good conversation, and people who showed up ready to meet each other.

A few quick thank-yous

  • Silent auction bidders

    To everyone who bid in the silent auction, we raised $345, and I will match that contribution to deliver $690 to Suncoast Voices for Children. Your generosity makes a real difference. If you would like to contribute further, you can make a donation here.

  • Our sponsors

    To each of our sponsors, thank you. This night would not have happened without your support. Meet them below.

  • Everyone who connected

    To everyone who moved from table to table and started a conversation with someone new. That is the whole point of the night, and you did it well.

  • Our host

    Also to Mike Alvarez for donating his hosting services and keeping the event on schedule.

One ask

Follow up this week

Here is my one ask: follow up this week. A short message to the people you met, even just "great to meet you, let's grab coffee," is what turns a good conversation into a real relationship.

If you met someone you would like me to introduce you to, send me a message and I will help make that happen.
