The format

How the night works

Our format is large table networking. Here's what to expect.

1

Arrive at 5:30

Doors open at 5:30 PM. Please arrive right at 5:30, since we'll be setting up before then. Settle in, grab a drink, and say hello. We're expecting 50+ people.

2

New table every round

Each guest sits with a new table of people every round, and a host at each table asks questions that ignite conversations. Be ready to make some friends and find good business opportunities.

3

Connect your way

Dinner and drinks are included, with a catered dinner and a large selection of adult drinks and non-alcoholic beverages. Exchange contact info with whoever you'd like. That part is entirely up to you.

The evening runs about 3 hours, wrapping up around 8:30 PM.

The cause

Connections that give back

Ignite Connections benefits Suncoast Voices for Children, a nonprofit that provides for the unmet needs of abused, abandoned, and neglected children in Pinellas and Pasco Counties.

Children's Needs

Urgently needed items and support for children's physical, emotional, educational, and social needs.

Style Voiced

Clothing for children in care, delivered with dignity.

Opportunity Passport

Financial training, coaching, and matched savings for young people who have been in foster care.

Learn more at suncoastvoices.org

Questions? Answered.

Good to know

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 5:30 PM. Please arrive at 5:30, since we'll be setting up before then.

How long is the event?

About 3 hours, from 5:30 to 8:30 PM.

Where do I park? Is it free?

Parking is free. There is parking directly in front of and beside the venue, plus plenty of street parking.

What's the dress code?

Semi-professional, stylish, or cocktail chic.

Are food and drinks included in my ticket?

Yes. Your dinner and drinks are included in your ticket price. We'll have a catered dinner and a large selection of adult drinks and non-alcoholic beverages.

How does speed networking actually work?

Our format is large table networking. Each guest sits with a new table of people every round, and a host at each table asks questions that ignite conversations. Be ready to make some friends and find good business opportunities.

How many people will I meet?

We're expecting 50+ people at the event.

What if I've never done this before?

No worries! It's a fun, easygoing event. You'll have a great time meeting new people, and you can always bring a friend.

Will I get contact info for the people I meet?

That's entirely up to you. Connect and exchange contact information with whoever you'd like.

Can I get a refund if I can't make it?

Refunds are handled through Eventbrite. Check the refund policy on the Eventbrite page, and reach out to us if you need help.

Is there a walk-in option, or do I have to register in advance?

If we are not fully sold out, we will accept walk-ups. To be safe, grab your ticket in advance on Eventbrite.

Can I donate an item to the silent auction?

Yes! Please contact Bonnie at savvyservicesfl@gmail.com.
